# Case Study: AI Brand Strategy

## Project Type
AI-Assisted Brand Strategy

## Business
Fictional skincare brand: Glow Theory

## Business Input
Industry: Skincare
Location: Pakistan
Audience: Women aged 20–35
Products: Face serum and moisturizer
Brand Personality: Minimal, trustworthy, modern
Goal: Improve brand awareness and online sales

## Problem
The brand needs a clear marketing direction
and consistent social media communication.

## AI Solution
Used the AI Brand Strategy Prompt to generate:

- Target audience analysis
- Customer pain points
- Brand positioning
- Content pillars
- Marketing recommendations

## Expected Output

Brand Positioning:
Affordable skincare with simple,
easy-to-understand product communication.

Content Pillars:
1. Skincare education
2. Product demonstrations
3. Customer questions and FAQs

## Human Review
Recommendations must be checked against
actual product information and customer research.

## Project Status
Demonstration using fictional business data.
